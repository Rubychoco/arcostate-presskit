About the game

Arcostate is a sci-fi roguelike set in a post-post-cataclysmic earth still bearing the scars that led to its ruin. Heavily inspired by the likes of Caves of Qud, Brogue, Elin, and others, this game aims to bring a little bit of everything together. Explore a ruined planet holding technological marvels that once promised a bright future for humanity.

You arrive in a foreign land, your memories gone and only your skills to aid you. The earth is scarred and hostile, evidence of a great cataclysm found in the many scattered ruins. Across to the south, a bright blue dome dominates the horizon. Aathman, the last surviving arcology on the planet, seemingly stands as a shining beacon that defends against the many dangers in the wastes.

Features

  • Turn-based sci-fi roguelike with deep simulation and emergent systems.
  • Classic mode for true permadeath, or roleplaying mode to save and load freely.
  • An overworld map with hand-authored and procedural locations, and caves that descend forever.
  • NPCs with full daily schedules, and a faction system with grudges and witness reactions.
  • Cybernetics gated by a compute-power budget. NPCs use them too, and augments can be cut out of corpses.
  • Psychic powers upgraded through the skill tree.
  • Drug crafting with a toxicity system, overdoses and withdrawals.
  • Companions, stealth driven by each NPC's perception, and momentum physics.
  • Claim land, build a home, or dig down and expand underground.
  • Over 400 items, including 48 weapons, 107 armor pieces and 46 cybernetics.
  • Points-based character creation with six attributes and multi-rank skills.
